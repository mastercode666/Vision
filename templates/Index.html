About Us

Call & Vision Secondary School is a missionary institution that emphasizes spiritual growth alongside academic excellence. We train students to be disciplined, socially responsible, and equipped with practical skills to impact their communities.

Our Mission

To nurture young people through Christ-centered education that develops character, leadership, and a lifelong calling for service.

Core Values
Faith Discipline Excellence Service Character

    Annual Volunteers Exchange

    Every year we host volunteers from Germany who join our teachers and students in cultural exchange, English language practice, and collaborative school projects. This program strengthens international relationships and broadens perspectives.

    • English conversation clubs and tutoring
    • Cultural workshops — food, music and traditions
    • Joint community service projects
